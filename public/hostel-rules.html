Residents returning after 22:00 must produce valid digital QR clearance. Three unexcused breaches trigger parental notification and biometric lock.

⚠️ Fine Schedule: 1st warning: ₹0 • 2nd: ₹25 • 3rd: Suspension from hostel amenities.

Overnight non-family guests are strictly prohibited inside resident blocks after 20:00. Guests are permitted only in the ground-floor cafeteria and common lounge.

🚫 Unauthorized overnight visitors incur immediate disciplinary hold and ₹50 fine.

Breakfast: 07:30 - 09:30 • Lunch: 12:30 - 14:30 • Dinner: 19:30 - 21:30. Residents must RSVP dinner opt-outs before 18:30 daily to avoid food wastage penalties.

Induction stoves, high-wattage space heaters (>1000W), and hot plates are banned in dorm rooms. Confiscated appliances are stored till semester conclusion.

Hostel fees are payable before the 5th of each calendar month. Unpaid accounts exceeding ₹500 result in automated turnstile deactivation after a 48h grace window.
